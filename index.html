Words you need (in plain English)
  • Function: a rule that takes a number in and gives one number out.
  • Domain: the numbers you are allowed to put in.
  • Range: the numbers that can come out.
  • Asymptote: a line the graph gets closer and closer to but never touches.
  • Composite function (such as fg): do g first, then feed the answer into f.
  • Inverse function: the function that undoes f, taking you back to where you started.
  • Tangent: a straight line that just touches a circle at one point.
  • Hypotenuse: the longest side of a right-angled triangle, opposite the right angle.
  • Exact value: leave it as a root or fraction (like 5√3), not a rounded decimal.
